\section{Modifications to chapter 8}

The following modifications shall be applied to chapter 8 (\emph{Statements}
[\texttt{stmt}]:

\begin{modification}[Modification of 8.8.4/5 Note 3]
NOTE 3 These operations are sequenced before the destruction of local variables in each remaining enclosing block of
the function (8.10), \stdremove{which, in turn, is sequenced before the
evaluation of postcondition assertions of the function (9.4.1),}
which, in turn, is sequenced before the destruction of function parameters (7.6.1.3).
\end{modification}

\begin{modification}[Removal of section 8.9]
Section 8.9 (\emph{Assertion statement} [\texttt{stmt.contract.assert}]) shall
be \textbf{completely removed}.
\end{modification}
