\section{Cost of contracts removal}

In contrast to the position stated in P4433R0~\cite{p4433r0}, we believe that
removing contracts is mostly an editorial change. It would consist of the
following actions:

\begin{itemize}

\item Removal of section 6.4.10 (\texttt{[basic.scope.contract]}).

\item Removal of section 6.11 (\texttt{[basic.contract]}).

\item Removal of section 8.9 (\texttt{[stmt.contract.assert]}).

\item Removal of section 9.4 (\texttt{[dcl.contract]}).

\item Removal of section 16.4.6.15 (\texttt{[res.contract.assertions]}).

\item Removal of section 17.10 (\texttt{[support.contract]}).

\item Removal of cross references to those sections.

\end{itemize}

Besides that, the specification of the hardened implementation needs to be
decoupled from contracts. That is not so complicated as it might seem, as the
initial specification of the hardened implementation did not include such
coupling.
