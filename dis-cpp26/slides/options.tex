\begin{frame}[t]{Options: Directives 2.6.4}
when the approval criteria of 2.6.3 are not met:
\begin{itemize}
  \item to circulate the revised enquiry draft for eight weeks voting (see 2.6.1), or
  \item to circulate the revised committee draft for comments, or
  \item to circulate a revised draft as a draft Technical Specification, or
  \item to cancel the project, subject to a decision by the committee.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Note:} These are the only options available under Directives 2.6.4.

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Recommendation}: We aim for option 1 with a revised draft that removes P2900 contracts from the standard.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Draft D4446R0 contains the wording for such removal.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
