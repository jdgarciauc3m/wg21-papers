According to other public sources~\cite{kowalska:lavx,ballot:hn,ballot:trainit}
the National Bodies voting against have been publicly reported. However, in
order to follow the \emph{ISO Policy on communication of Committee
Work}~\cite{iso:comm:policy} the identification of specific votes is not
reported here. The interested reader can get that information from those
sources.

Some preliminary observations are worth to be done:

\begin{itemize}
  \item The opposition to contracts has increased from the CD stage to the DIS
stage.

  \item While the CD received requests from different National Bodies to
remove contracts, the DIS has received 9 negative votes. The number of requests
to remove contracts has increased.

  \item The number of National Bodies that issued a vote different from Yes
(either abstain or negative) is quite high. This reflects opposition to the
current proposal as well as the difficulties in many National Bodies to achieve a
consensus decision.

  \item The lack of consensus affects National Bodies where many proponents of
the contracts feature belong to.

\end{itemize}

While the goal of ballot resolution after the CD stage is to increase consensus,
it seems that in this specific case that goal was not achieved. In fact, it
seems that consensus has decreased.
