Some public sources~\cite{kowalska:lavx,ballot:hn,ballot:trainit} identify
individual National Bodies. This paper does not reproduce those identities,
consistent with the \emph{ISO Policy on communication of Committee
Work}~\cite{iso:comm:policy}.

Some preliminary observations are worth noting:

\begin{itemize}
\item The accompanying summary records six National Bodies marking ``Remove''
for contracts at the CD stage. The DIS ballot received nine negative votes;
these are different measures, so this comparison alone does not establish
whether opposition to contracts increased.

\item Of the 34 recorded responses, 13 were Yes, 9 were No, and 12 were
abstentions. Abstentions are distinct from negative votes and should not be
attributed to opposition to contracts without examining the reasons given.

\end{itemize}

The DIS was not approved. Because this paper does not provide comparable CD-stage
vote totals or define a measure of consensus, these results alone do not show
whether consensus increased or decreased.
