Some preliminary observations are worth to be done:

\begin{itemize}
  \item None of the NBs that requested the removal of the contracts feature in
the CD stage has voted in favour of the DIS.

  \item While the CD received requests from 6 different National Bodies to
remove contracts, the DIS has received 9 negative votes all of them specifically requesting to remove the contracts feature. Moreover 2 abstaining countries have also requested in theircomments to remove that feature.

  \item In total, there are comments requesting to remove the contracts feature
from 11 different National Bodies.

  \item The number of abstains is surprisingly high. For C++23, a total of 3
National Bodies abstained. In contrast with that, for C++26 a total of 12
National Bodies have abstained.

  \item The number of National Bodies that issued a vote different from Yes
(either abstain or negative) is quite high. This reflects opposition to the
current proposal and also difficulties in many National Bodies to achieve a
consensus decision.

  \item It is quite surprising that the National Body where many of the main
proponents of the contract feature belong to had serious difficulties to achieve
consensus to support the DIS and finally decided to abstain.

\end{itemize}

While the goal of ballot resolution after the CD stage is to increase consensus,
it seems that in this specific case that goal was not achieved. In fact, it
seems that consensus has decreased.
