\begin{abstract}
After the conclusion of the ballot period for the DIS of what was expected to be
C++26, it is now clear that the ballot failed and the DIS has been disapproved.
A proposed resolution is needed as a response. In this paper, we analyze current
options and I make a recommendation to make sure that most of the features are
included in the ISO/IEC 14882 standard as soon as possible.
\end{abstract}
