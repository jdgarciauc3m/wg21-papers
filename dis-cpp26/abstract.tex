\begin{abstract}
After the conclusion of the ballot period for the 14882 DIS of what was expected
to be the C++26 Standard, it is now clear that the ballot failed and the DIS has
been disapproved. A proposed resolution is needed as a response. In this paper,
we analyze current options and we make a recommendation ensuring that most
of the features are included in the ISO/IEC 14882 Standard as soon as possible.
\end{abstract}
