\section{Recommendation}

We very strongly recommend that the contracts feature is removed from the DIS
and a new ballot is initiated as soon as possible. This will allow to deliver
Edition 8 of ISO/IEC 14882 almost in time with most of the features.

In parallel with that WG21 consider one of the following options:

\begin{enumerate}

\item
To start a White Paper on contracts. This option would even allow to include
multiple competing solutions.

\item
To start working on a revised design of contracts for Edition 9 of ISO/IEC 14882
(hopefully C++29).

\end{enumerate}
