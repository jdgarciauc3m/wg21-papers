\section{Recommendation}

We strongly recommend removing the contracts feature from the DIS and
circulating a revised enquiry draft for an eight-week ballot as soon as
possible. This would offer a path to publishing Edition 8 of ISO/IEC 14882 with
most of its planned features and minimal delay.

In parallel, WG21 should begin work on a minimal contracts solution for Edition
9 of ISO/IEC 14882 (hopefully C++29). That solution should include only
features for which strong consensus can be achieved both in WG21 and among the
National Bodies.
